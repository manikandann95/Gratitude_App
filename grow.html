21 days · 6 foundation practices

The first bundle for anyone new to manifestation. Most beginners try to manifest by jumping straight to visualization or affirmations — and quit in two weeks because nothing shifts. The reason is simple: manifestation is not one practice. It is three layers working together. This 21-day bundle gives you all three: Mindset — what you tell yourself every day (Daily Affirmation, Vision Minute). Emotional state — how you bookend your day (Gratitude Writing, morning and night). Energetic clearing — what you stop consuming (Media Detox, People Detox). Twenty-one days is long enough to feel the shift. Short enough to commit to. What you will do each day: Six small practices. None of them take more than a few minutes. The whole routine fits inside 15 minutes a day — most of it before you leave your bed.
